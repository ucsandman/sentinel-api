## Critical Alerts

- **New York comment window closes 14 September 2026.** NYDFS proposed rules on 23 February 2026 to implement the state's Buy-Now-Pay-Later Act, and comments on the State Register version are due 14 September. This is the last cheap moment to shape the rule. ([NYDFS proposal analysis, Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/03/New-York-Proposes-Comprehensive-BNPL-Rule-What-BNPL-Providers-Need-to-Know), [Cooley Finsights](https://finsights.cooley.com/nydfs-proposes-rules-to-enact-buy-now-pay-later-statute/))

- **The New York cap is all-in, not a rate cap.** The proposal sets 16% inclusive of origination charges, Regulation Z finance charges, and anything characterized as a fee that functions as interest. A pay-in-four product with a $7 late fee can breach a 16% all-in ceiling while carrying a stated APR of zero. Model your fee stack, not your rate card. ([Davis Wright Tremaine](https://www.dwt.com/blogs/financial-services-law-advisor/2026/03/ny-unveils-sweeping-bnpl-regulatory-framework))

- **US federal and US state policy are now moving in opposite directions.** The CFPB withdrew its 2024 BNPL interpretive rule on 12 May 2025, after saying on 6 May 2025 that it would not prioritize enforcement under it. That rule had treated pay-in-four lenders as credit card issuers under Regulation Z. Federal retreat did not reduce exposure. It moved exposure to the states. ([CFPB newsroom](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-announcement-regarding-enforcement-actions-related-to-buy-now-pay-later-loans/), [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/05/cfpb-provides-status-update-regarding-buy-now-pay-later))

## Regulatory Pulse

- **UK: FCA rules bind from 15 July 2026.** Deferred payment credit lenders need FCA authorisation, must comply with the Consumer Duty, and must run proportionate affordability assessments before extending credit. Point-of-sale disclosure of the payment schedule, amounts due, and missed-payment consequences becomes mandatory. ([Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102mmmf/new-fca-regulation-of-buy-now-pay-later-what-you-need-to-know/), [PKF Littlejohn](https://www.pkf-l.com/insights/fca-bnpl-regulation-2026-rules-timeline-compliance/))

- **The UK temporary permissions window has already closed.** Registration ran 15 May 2026 to 1 July 2026, with six months from commencement to submit a full authorisation pack. If a UK entity did not register in that window, it is not in the queue, and that is a live problem right now, not a 2027 problem. ([The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/regulations/news/fca-confirms-new-protections-for-bnpl-borrowers))

- **New York licensing has a 45-day trap.** Once the rules are adopted they take effect 180 days later, but existing lenders get only 45 days from that effective date to file for a license and category permission in order to keep operating while the application is pending. The 180 days is not the deadline. The 45 days is. ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/from-statute-to-supervision-preparing-for-new-yorks-new-bnpl))

- **Federal penalty exposure did not disappear.** CFPB civil penalties remain tiered under 12 U.S.C. 5565(c)(2) at $7,217, $36,083 and $1,443,275 per day for tiers 1 to 3, applying to penalties assessed after 15 January 2025. OMB guidance of 17 April 2026 means there is no 2026 inflation adjustment, so the 2025 figures stand through 14 January 2027. ([12 CFR 1083.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/1083.1), [Federal Register, no 2026 adjustment](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/07/2026-13629/no-adjustment-to-civil-monetary-penalty-amounts))

## Key Questions

1. **Does your fee stack survive an all-in 16% ceiling?** Run the New York math on your worst-case borrower, not your average one. A late fee plus an expedited payment fee on a small basket is where the ceiling breaks first.

2. **Who owns the 45-day New York filing, by name?** The 180-day runway invites teams to schedule this for later. The operative deadline is 45 days after that, and it gates whether you can keep lending in the state at all.

3. **If federal retreat is temporary, what did you dismantle?** Compliance capability built for the 2024 interpretive rule is expensive to rebuild. New York, and the states that copy it, need most of the same machinery.
